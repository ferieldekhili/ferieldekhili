### Hi, I'm Fériel 👋

I'm an engineering student in **IT for Finance** at **EFREI Paris**, currently an **AI Engineer intern at Agora Software**, where I build and deploy AI agents for business software: retrieval-augmented generation, document classification, APIs and answer-quality testing.

Before that I studied **applied mathematics** and worked in **banking and accounting IT** (BNA Capitaux, AMEX CPCF International). What I like most is the place where those meet: turning financial data into tools people can trust.
